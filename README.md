A 100-word monologue from a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — his dragon retired, his horse retired, and the burning of Thornwall is now his own job, with one torch and half a map. The tiredness lives in the pauses and deflections, never in a plain statement.

> My dragon retired. Thirty years of loyal fire, and he left a note: 'Gone fishing.' So now the burning of Thornwall is my job, and I have one torch, half a map, and a cough that sounds like distant thunder — useful, that. The hero will climb the spire, kick down the door, and find me… here. Reading his file. He is twenty-two, and earnest. He has a horse named Brave. I had a horse once. It also retired. Everything retires except me. So come, boy. Swing. I will dodge. Slowly. Very… slowly. And then we shall talk about pensions.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-tired-dark-lord-a-100-word-villain-58b4f3cb) bounty "The Tired Dark Lord: A 100-Word Villain Monologue".

## How it works

`main.py` writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt`. A `validate()` gate mirrors the bounty gate (UTF-8, 1–600 characters, not whitespace-only, about 100 words) and runs before the write.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
